# Creative direction — Meet EdulabsAI

## Campaign promise

EdulabsAI is not another isolated lesson generator. It is the connected teaching
workspace that carries one learning intention through planning, classroom
resources, live teaching, student work, evidence, reteaching, and family
communication.

## Visual language

- Aubergine and cream establish the EdulabsAI product world.
- Coral-orange is reserved for motion, calls to action, and moments of progress.
- Gold and teal identify generated resources and verified evidence.
- Real product footage is shown in stable, readable frames. It never shakes,
  continuously zooms, or moves while the viewer is expected to read it.
- Animated lines, nodes, cards, and orbiting dots communicate connection. Motion
  uses short 0.35–0.55 second entrances with calm holds between them.
- Landscape and vertical compositions are authored separately. Vertical exports
  place the product inside a readable device frame rather than cropping desktop
  UI into an unreadable strip.

## Sound

- Neural narration uses the cheerful, clear Emma multilingual voice for the
  campaign master. Scripts target 118–132 words per minute.
- `Connected Spark` is an original 124 BPM electronic score generated inside the
  campaign. It has a restrained opening, rhythmic build, evidence breakdown,
  and resolved final lift.
- Original UI clicks, whooshes, sparkles, and impact cues mark visual events.
- Agent Demo Studio ducks the music beneath speech and masters final output to
  platform-safe loudness and true-peak targets.

## Primary launch-film arc

1. Meet EdulabsAI.
2. Teaching is a loop, not a pile of disconnected files.
3. One curriculum-aware brief becomes a plan, slides, quiz, and worksheet.
4. Visual teaching resources make concepts visible.
5. Students move from live teaching into focused work and managed collaboration.
6. Paper and digital evidence return as a clear next teaching move.
7. Teacher-approved progress reaches families without breaking the learning loop.
8. Plan. Teach. Check. Reteach. Connect.

## Quality rules

- No unlicensed music, stock footage, logos, or people.
- No unsupported performance or outcome claims.
- No passwords, tokens, real student identities, or private school data.
- Every product claim must be visibly supported by verified browser footage.
- Captions must remain inside platform safe zones and never cover primary UI.
- Every final export must pass dimension, codec, frame-rate, audio, caption,
  black-frame, and representative-frame review before delivery.
