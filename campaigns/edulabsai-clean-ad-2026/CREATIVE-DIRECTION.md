# Creative direction — One learning loop

The advertisement should feel calm, premium, human, and intentionally designed. The visual system uses EdulabsAI cream, aubergine, coral, gold, teal, and lilac on a strict 96-pixel safe grid.

## Non-negotiable stability rules

1. Every scene background is a 1920×1080 lossless PNG with `motion: none`.
2. Every reveal element is a transparent 1920×1080 layer placed at the exact canvas center and faded only.
3. Product footage is represented by high-resolution still frames; no product video loops are used.
4. All text and artwork remain above the caption-safe line at y=900.
5. Scene changes use `cut`; no layout is transformed between scenes.
6. Overlay loop animation is always `none`.
7. The audio manifest contains an empty sound-effects list.
8. The score has no percussion, risers, impacts, clicks, sparkles, or intermittent cues.
9. The single motion exception is the opening orange EdulabsAI mark, which drops once into its fixed center position and never moves again.

## Layout grid

- Canvas: 1920×1080
- Safe margins: 96 pixels on all sides
- Brand baseline: y=64
- Primary title zone: y=170–430
- Product/art zone: y=330–880
- Caption exclusion zone: y=900–1080
- Single product card: 1040×585
- Dual product cards: 790×444 each

## Story

The story begins with one teaching idea, moves through classroom context and a four-resource lesson package, shows teaching and student work, returns evidence to a focused reteach, connects approved progress to families, and closes with a simple free-beta call to action.
