# Image asset provenance

The three campaign illustrations were produced with OpenAI's built-in image-generation mode and then used as visual anchors inside original project-local layouts. They depict fictional people only. No reference image, real identity, third-party logo, readable interface text, or learner data was requested.

## `teacher-fragmented.png`

Final prompt:

> Create a premium cinematic editorial illustration in a tactile paper-cut and soft clay 3D style. Show an original fictional South Asian woman teacher in her early thirties, warm medium-brown skin, shoulder-length wavy black hair, wearing a coral cardigan over a deep plum blouse, seated at a desk and looking capable but overwhelmed. Surround her with many disconnected generic lesson documents, slide cards, quiz sheets, worksheet pages, browser panels, and duplicated task windows, with broken connector lines that make the workflow feel fragmented. Use a rich aubergine classroom-studio background, warm coral and amber rim light, small teal accents, polished advertising lighting, clean depth, expressive but believable hands, and a centered square composition with generous edge room for motion-design cropping. No readable text, no logos, no real person, no watermark.

Saved as `assets/generated/illustrations/teacher-fragmented.png`.

## `classroom-connected.png`

Final prompt:

> Create a premium cinematic editorial classroom illustration in the same tactile paper-cut and soft clay 3D style. Feature the same original fictional South Asian woman teacher in a coral cardigan and plum blouse at the front of a warm modern classroom, guiding a small diverse group of fictional middle-school learners shown mostly from behind or in three-quarter view. Connect a lesson visual, student practice cards, progress evidence, and a family communication card with one elegant coral line so the scene feels collaborative and human. Use aubergine, coral, amber, warm cream, and restrained teal; polished advertising light; expressive natural poses; and a centered square composition with space for motion-design cropping. No readable text, no logos, no real identities, no watermark.

Saved as `assets/generated/illustrations/classroom-connected.png`.

The final rendered scenes add code-native typography, brand assets, product captures, connector drawings, masks, and VFX. Those additions were not baked into the generated source illustrations.
