# Creative research notes

The campaign translates current official short-form and video-ad guidance into a teacher-specific story rather than copying an existing advertisement.

## Sources and decisions

- [Google Ads ABCDs of effective video ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/14783551?hl=en): informed the immediate human problem, tight framing, early branding, high contrast, voice-led story, tangible product proof, and direct close.
- [Google Ads creative guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/13812351?hl=en): informed the prominent brand presence during the opening seconds, human narration, and separately authored aspect-ratio variants.
- [Google Ads vertical-video guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9128498?hl=en-gb): informed the independent full-screen 9:16 master rather than shrinking or cropping the landscape layout.
- [Meta Reels ads guidance](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads): informed the 9:16 composition, audio-led delivery, and placement of key information inside mobile interface safe zones.
- [TikTok Creative Codes](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/TikTok_CreativeCodes_May2023.pdf): informed the hook–body–close structure and the problem-to-solution turn.
- [TikTok Creative Codes production principles](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/Creative_Codes_ENG.pdf): informed high-resolution vertical production and deliberate safe-space margins.

## Resulting campaign rules

1. Lead with the teacher's emotional cost before the feature list.
2. Make the orange EdulabsAI mark dominant during the opening and retain a small brand anchor afterward.
3. Use original fictional people to humanize the story without exposing real identities.
4. Show product proof in large, task-specific crops instead of unreadable full-dashboard miniatures.
5. Author landscape and vertical layouts independently.
6. Keep captions, headlines, product detail, and CTA inside measured safe zones.
7. End with one plain-language benefit and one direct action.
