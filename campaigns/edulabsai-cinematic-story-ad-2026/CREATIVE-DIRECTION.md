# Creative direction

This campaign is a teacher story first and a product proof second. It opens with an oversized EdulabsAI brand arrival, immediately shows the friction of working tool by tool, and then turns the same visual language into one connected learning loop.

The visual system combines original fictional teacher/classroom artwork, code-native paper-cut drawings, tightly cropped verified product interfaces, large kinetic type, and restrained connection-line VFX. Backgrounds do not drift or shake. Each animation happens once, settles, and gives the viewer time to read.

The landscape master is authored natively at 3840 x 2160. The vertical master is authored independently at 1080 x 1920 with large central subjects and mobile safe zones; it is not a crop of the 4K composition.

The research basis is the current official platform guidance: hook quickly, show the brand early and prominently, humanize the story, make the product tangible, pair voice with readable supers, use native vertical creative, and end with a direct call to action.
