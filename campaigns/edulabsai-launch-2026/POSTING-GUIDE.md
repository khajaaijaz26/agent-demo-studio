# Posting and campaign guide

## Recommended rollout

- **Week 1 — awareness:** publish `reel-01`, `reel-02` and the Connected Classroom Tour. Lead with the time-saving and visual-quality hooks.
- **Week 2 — trust:** publish `reel-03`, `reel-04` and Evidence to Action. Retarget engaged viewers with teacher-control and review-first messaging.
- **Week 3 — conversion:** publish `reel-05`, Curriculum to Classroom and the School Pilot video. Use “Create your first lesson” for teachers and “Book a school pilot” for leaders.
- Recut hooks only after each asset has enough impressions to compare three-second hold, completion rate, click-through and landing-page conversion.

## Platform use

- Full videos: YouTube, LinkedIn, Facebook video, school-demo follow-up email and the EdulabsAI media page.
- Reels: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Facebook Reels. The included 1080×1920 render keeps captions away from common interface overlays.
- Upload the supplied sidecar SRT wherever supported even though captions are already burned into the picture. This improves accessibility and platform indexing.
