# Campaign plan

## Four full videos

1. **Connected classroom tour** — the complete curriculum → resources → live teaching → evidence → reteach → family loop.
2. **Curriculum to classroom** — generation, full subject coverage, editable visuals, preview, export and live delivery.
3. **Evidence to action** — Snap and Mark review, verified misconceptions, Tomorrow Packs and Family Digests.
4. **School pilot** — a leadership-oriented story about connected workflow, teacher controls and safe adoption.

## Five reels

1. **Two minutes to ready** — a time-saving acquisition hook for teachers.
2. **Subject visuals** — fractions, science processes, geometry and other topic-aware diagrams.
3. **Paper to next step** — controlled paper recognition through to a focused reteach.
4. **Safe class connection** — class and group chat, admins, focus locking and family communication.
5. **One connected loop** — the entire product promise in a vertical, sub-minute story.

All nine assets open with the coral/orange EdulabsAI mark, end with the EdulabsAI name and website, use the same sunrise palette, include neural narration and music, and carry burned captions within conservative platform safe zones.
